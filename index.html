I am a graduate research assistant, teaching assistant and a Masters graduate in Artificial Intelligence from Carnegie Mellon University's College of Engineering. My research focuses on deep learning for medical imaging, computer vision and Generative AI.

Prior to CMU, I earned a Bachelor's degree (First class Honors) in Software Engineering from The ICT University in Cameroon, graduating first in my department. I am currently working with the AI Healthcare Research lab at CMU, to understand the heart and revolutionizing how cardiac health is assessed by simultaneously acquiring and analyzing electrocardiogram (ECG) and phonocardiogram (PCG) signals using machine learning.

Beyond work, I enjoy traveling, reading, and watching documentaries.
