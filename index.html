I am a research associate, teaching assistant, and Masters graduate in Artificial Intelligence from Carnegie Mellon University's College of Engineering. My research focuses on using machine learning to process, understand, and make inferences from signals in the world around us, with a particular interest in visual and multimodal representations.

Prior to CMU, I earned a Bachelor's degree with First Class Honors in Software Engineering from The ICT University in Cameroon, graduating first in my department. I currently work with the AI Healthcare Research Lab at CMU, where I work on machine learning methods for understanding cardiac health through the joint analysis of electrocardiogram (ECG) and phonocardiogram (PCG) signals.

Beyond work, I enjoy traveling, reading, and watching documentaries.
